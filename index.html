About Sarah

A practice built around one relationship at a time.

I’m Sarah Whitfield, a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC-S) with a solo practice in Dallas. For the past nine years I’ve worked with adults, teens, and children carrying OCD, anxiety, depression, and the everyday weight of hard seasons.

My approach blends Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) for OCD and anxiety, cognitive-behavioral therapy, and — with younger clients — play-based and expressive techniques that meet kids where language runs out.

Because it’s just me, there’s no hand-off between an intake coordinator and a rotating cast of clinicians. You’ll work with the same person who read your first message, from the free consultation call through however long we work together.

  • Licensed Professional Counselor – Supervisor (LPC-S), Texas
  • Certified in Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) for OCD
  • Trained in child-centered play therapy
  • 9 years in private practice; formerly with a Dallas community counseling center

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept insurance?

I’m private pay and not in-network with any insurance panels, which lets me keep caseloads small and sessions consistent. I provide a superbill you can submit to your insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement — many PPO plans cover a portion.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. I see clients in-person in Dallas and via secure telehealth video anywhere in Texas, where I’m licensed to practice.

What ages do you work with?

Children age 6 and up, teens, and adults. Sessions with younger kids usually involve a parent for at least part of the intake.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you’re working on — some anxiety and adjustment work moves in 8–12 sessions; OCD treatment with ERP is often longer. We’ll talk about a rough timeline after the consultation call.
